[ABOUT]

Aaron Mendelson is a journalist based in Los Angeles. He specializes in data and investigative reporting.

His award-winning work has spanned beats, with an emphasis on finding the untold stories buried inside databases and public records.

[WORK]

Aaron works at The Trace. Previously, he worked at the Center for Public Integrity and KPCC. His work has also appeared in USA Today, The Guardian, GQ, NPR and elsewhere.

His stories include a deep dive into the awesome power of state supreme courts, an investigation into a hidden empire of rental housing, and a look at the crisis of suicide among older Americans.
